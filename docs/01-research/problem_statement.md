# Problem Statement

## Overview

Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) form the backbone of the manufacturing industry. However, many of these businesses still rely on reactive or schedule-based maintenance strategies to manage their machinery.

In reactive maintenance, machines are repaired only after they fail. This leads to unexpected production downtime, increased repair costs, delayed deliveries, and reduced operational efficiency.

Preventive maintenance improves reliability by servicing machines at regular intervals, but it often results in unnecessary maintenance because machines may still be in good condition.

Enterprise predictive maintenance solutions offered by large vendors are expensive, require specialized infrastructure, and are often beyond the budget of MSMEs.

As a result, many small and medium manufacturers continue operating without real-time visibility into machine health.

---

## Problem

MSMEs need an affordable, intelligent, and easy-to-use solution that can monitor machine health, identify abnormal behavior, and predict failures before they occur.

Without such a system, businesses face:

* Unexpected machine breakdowns
* High maintenance costs
* Production delays
* Reduced machine lifespan
* Poor maintenance planning
* Financial losses due to downtime

---

## Proposed Solution

MACHINASENSE is an AI-powered Manufacturing Intelligence Platform designed specifically for MSMEs.

The platform analyzes machine sensor data to:

* Monitor machine health
* Detect anomalies
* Predict potential failures
* Estimate machine health scores
* Assist maintenance teams with proactive decision-making

The initial version of MACHINASENSE will support CSV-based sensor data uploads, making the platform accessible even to businesses that do not yet have IoT infrastructure.

Future versions will integrate live IoT sensors, real-time monitoring, AI-driven maintenance recommendations, and advanced analytics.

---

## Expected Impact

By implementing MACHINASENSE, manufacturing industries can:

* Reduce unplanned downtime
* Improve production efficiency
* Lower maintenance costs
* Extend machine life
* Increase operational visibility
* Begin their Industry 4.0 transformation with an affordable solution
