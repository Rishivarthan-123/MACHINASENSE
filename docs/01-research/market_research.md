# Market Research

# Overview

The manufacturing sector is one of the largest contributors to the global economy. As industries move towards Industry 4.0, manufacturers are increasingly adopting digital technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), Cloud Computing, and Data Analytics to improve productivity and reduce operational costs.

Large enterprises have already invested in predictive maintenance systems, while many Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) still depend on traditional maintenance practices due to budget limitations.

This creates a significant opportunity for an affordable AI-powered predictive maintenance platform.

---

# Industry Overview

Manufacturing companies experience losses due to:

* Unexpected machine failures
* Production downtime
* High maintenance costs
* Poor maintenance scheduling
* Equipment degradation

Traditional maintenance methods include:

* Reactive Maintenance
* Preventive Maintenance

Modern industries are moving toward:

* Predictive Maintenance
* Condition-Based Monitoring
* AI-powered Analytics
