# Customer Persona

## Primary Customer

### Name

Rajesh Kumar (Fictional Persona)

### Age

42 Years

### Occupation

Factory Owner

### Industry

Textile Manufacturing (MSME)

### Company Size

* 40 Employees
* 25 Industrial Machines
* 2 Maintenance Technicians

---

## Background

Rajesh owns a medium-sized textile manufacturing unit in Tamil Nadu. His factory operates for nearly 16 hours every day to meet customer demands.

Unexpected machine failures frequently interrupt production, resulting in delayed deliveries and increased maintenance costs.

He cannot afford expensive enterprise solutions from companies like Siemens or IBM but wants a modern and affordable system to improve machine maintenance.

---

## Goals

* Reduce machine downtime
* Increase production efficiency
* Monitor all machines from one dashboard
* Receive alerts before machine failures
* Reduce maintenance expenses
* Improve machine lifespan

---

## Pain Points

* No real-time machine monitoring
* Maintenance is mostly reactive
* Unexpected machine breakdowns
* High repair costs
* Lack of data for decision making
* Production delays due to equipment failure

---

## Technical Skills

* Basic computer knowledge
* Uses Excel for reports
* Uses WhatsApp for communication
* Limited knowledge of AI and IoT

The software should therefore be simple, intuitive, and easy to use.

---

## Daily Workflow

1. Starts factory operations.
2. Monitors production targets.
3. Handles machine failures when reported.
4. Calls maintenance technicians.
5. Reviews production reports.
6. Plans maintenance manually.

---

## Expectations from MACHINASENSE

Rajesh expects the platform to:

* Show the health status of every machine.
* Predict failures before they happen.
* Notify him immediately when a machine is at risk.
* Help schedule maintenance efficiently.
* Reduce production downtime.
* Provide clear reports without requiring AI expertise.

---

## Quote

> "I don't need complicated AI. I just need to know which machine is likely to fail and what action I should take before it stops production."
